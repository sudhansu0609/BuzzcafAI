# Reels Desk: Instagram-first short videos made end to end by the tool

Status: planned 2026-10-23 (the creator's request that night). Owner: Studio
`app/services/reels_desk.py` + BuzzEdit's recording-less render path.
Update the Status column in §5 after every item.

## 1. What the creator asked for

Two Instagram-first products, 15-45 s, vertical, everything local, nothing
recorded by him:

- **Life3Baje mood reels**: soothing hyper-realistic (or painterly) scenes --
  mountains, a street lamp in rain, curtains in wind, a night road -- with
  2-4 real ghazal lines on screen and a soothing bed. No voice.
- **Raat3Baje horror reels**: a short horror story, rules horror, an SCP-style
  anomaly file or an entity file, narrated. The narration is generated by a
  text-to-speech voice and converted to HIS voice with the SkinWalker voice
  changer already inside BuzzEdit (`audio/voice_conversion.py`, seed-vc), with
  good intonation; he may also give a base audio or his own voice instead.

Posting is mostly Instagram, so the deliverable is the vertical file plus a
caption and hashtags, and later an Instagram Graph API upload.

and the **desk page**.

## 3. The pipeline

```
Reels desk (Studio)                                   BuzzEdit
  1 pick: channel, format, length (15/30/45 s), style
  2 words: ghazal lines (Life3Baje) | script (Raat3Baje: story/rules/anomaly/entity)
  3 plan: N portrait shots in one world, no people on Life3Baje, no camera words
  4 narration (Raat3Baje): TTS in a chosen voice -> seed-vc to his voice ----> audio/narration.wav
  5 bed: ACE-Step mood (or his base audio) + scene SFX                      --> audio/bed.wav
  6 pictures: Z-Image portrait stills -> Wan clips (or slow push on stills) --> clips/
  7 render: recording-less timeline (clips + cards + narration + bed), 9:16 -> <name>_reel.mp4
  8 package: caption, hashtags, cover frame; Instagram upload later (Graph API)
```

Timing per 30 s reel on the 5060 Ti: Wan clips ~25 min, or ~2 min with stills
and a push; narration + conversion ~1 min; render ~1 min.

## 4. Decisions (defaults unless the creator says otherwise)

- **TTS engine**: probed first (§5 R1) in its own venv `BuzzEdit/engines/tts/`
  (never the backend venv): Chatterbox Multilingual (MIT, Hindi, emotion /
  exaggeration control) as the candidate, Kokoro (Apache, Hindi voices, fast)
  as the fallback. XTTS-v2 is excluded: non-commercial licence. Hinglish in
  Roman script is transliterated to Devanagari for the engine; English words
  stay English.
- **His voice**: a 30-60 s clean reference cut from a Raat3Baje recording,
  registered in `data/voices` as `sudhansu`; every generated narration is
  converted to it before the mix. His own recording, when given, skips 4.
- **Styles**: `real` for hyper-realistic; a new `painterly` art style (soft
  oil / gouache, muted, no outlines) for the illustration reels.
- **Life3Baje reels have no voice and no face**; Raat3Baje reels have no face,
  no captions by default (story-channel rule), captions as a toggle for
  Instagram's sound-off viewers.
- **Music**: generated or the creator's own file. Instagram-library songs are
  added inside the Instagram app by him, never by the tool.

## 6. Vibe reels (Life3Baje, 2026-10-23 evening)

The creator: "good soothing videos of moonlight, landscapes, storms, cozy vibes
... good 2-4 lines, deep, emotional, relatable, Hindi or Urdu ghazals on it. No
extra editing, just vibes."

The first mood reel (`2026-10-23_Life3Baje_MoodReelWaqt235d`) went through the
talking-video presentation pipeline and came out wrong: no scenes (dark
placeholder frames), an English heading, a text card that was not the ghazal, a
popup. So a mood reel no longer goes through the production queue / presentation
planner: it has its own plain renderer in BuzzEdit.

- **Look**: 4-6 portrait scenes in one world (moonlight, misty mountains, a
  storm over fields, rain on a window with a lamp and tea, a quiet night road,
  snow, a lake at dusk), each a Wan clip with natural motion (rain, clouds,
  water, curtains), 1 s dissolves, nothing else. No zooms, no transitions, no
  headings, no captions, no popups, no logo. No voice.
- **Words**: 2-4 lines (whole couplets) from a public-domain Hindi/Urdu poet
  (died before 1966: Ghalib, Mir, Momin, Daagh, Jigar, Majaz, Iqbal, Kabir,
  Meera, Nirala, Prasad ...), picked for being relatable today, fetched from the
  web (never recalled). The poem may be in any language (Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi ...);
  on screen it is written in Hindi (Devanagari) or Hinglish (Roman letters), never
  Urdu script (creator, 2026-10-23). Shown one couplet at a time, soft fade,
  white with a soft shadow, the poet's name small under the last one.
- **Sound**: a generated soothing bed (ACE-Step) + the scene's ambience (rain,
  wind, far thunder, crickets) under it; fades in and out; -14 LUFS.
- **Length**: 20-30 s (each couplet ~6-8 s on screen).
