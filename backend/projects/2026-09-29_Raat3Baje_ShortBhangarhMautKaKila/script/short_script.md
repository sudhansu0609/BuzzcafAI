# Bhangarh: Maut ka Kila 🏰

Rajasthan ka Bhangarh Fort... ek aisa naam jo sunte hi darr ki leher daud jaati hai. (pause) Ise India ka 'most haunted' kila maana jata hai, aur iski wajah sirf purani kahaniyan nahi, balki yahan ke kade niyam hain. (normal)

Kehte hain... suryast ke baad is kile ke andar kadam rakhna maut ko dawat dene jaisa hai. Local log aur authorities ise ek 'forbidden zone' maante hain. Log puchte hain ki itne saare mandiron ke beech bhoot kaise reh sakte hain? (pause) Lekin sacchai ye hai ki yahan ka mahol hi kuch alag hai. (normal)

Ek baar ek group ne is kile ki talash mein safar kiya. Jaise-jaise wo Bhangarh kareeb pahunche, mausam achanak badalne laga. Aasman par kaale baadal chha gaye aur dhoop gayab ho gayi, jaise koi shakti unhe rasta dikhana band kar rahi ho. (pause) Jab wo andar pahunche, toh unhone dekha ki yahan sirf khandhar nahi hain, balki ek ajeeb si khamoshi hai jo kaanon ko chubhti hai. (normal)

Wo log 'Bhoot Bangla' ke naam se mashhoor is jagah par ghoom rahe the, lekin piche mudkar dekhne par unhe ehsas hua ki sannate mein kuch aur bhi maujood hai. (pause) Bhangarh sirf ek kila nahi, balki sadiyon purani ek aisi dastan hai jise waqt ne rok diya hai. (beat) Kya aap himmat karenge yahan suryast ke baad rukne ki? (slow) Kuch jagahon par jana maut ko bulawa dena hota hai.
