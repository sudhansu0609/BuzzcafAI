Raat ke sawa gyarah baje, ek aadmi apni nayi zameen mein foundation khod raha tha jab phawde ko kuch lakdi jaisa mehsoos hua — aur ek purani awaaz goonji: 'Peeche mat khodna.' Ye hai 'Peeche Mat Khodna: The Forbidden Boundary,' Raat3Baje ki nayi Hinglish horror story jisme ek Mumbai se aaya parivaar apne pushtaini gaon ki zameen par ghar banata hai, bina jaane ki wo zameen saalon se khaali kyun padi thi.

Kahani khatam hote hi, hum enter karte hain real investigation mein — Gram Devta ki raksha parampara, Pishach aur Pret ki lok-kathaon ki, Masaan aur ashudh zameen ke vishwas ki, aur Raksha Kavach jaisi Tantric sealing rituals ki. Hum ye bhi dekhenge ki Rajasthan ke purane kuye se lekar Kerala ke sacred groves (Kavu) tak, har region ka apna 'forbidden ground' version kaise hai.

Ye kahani poori tarah FICTIONAL hai — koi real family, koi real gaon nahi — lekin iske peeche ki parampara bilkul asli hai.
