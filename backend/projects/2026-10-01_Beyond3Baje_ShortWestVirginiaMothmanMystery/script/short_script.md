# West Virginia ka Khauf: The Mothman 👁️

West Virginia... Point Pleasant. (pause) 1960s ke end mein, yahan ki ek purani kahani ne duniya bhar ke log ko hairan kar diya. Ek aisi entity jisne logon ko darane se zyada, unhe 'aane wale khatre' ka sanket diya. (normal)
