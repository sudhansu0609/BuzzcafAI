# Brand Style Guide: Khayal3Baje

## Overview
Khayal3Baje is the premium horror, folklore, and supernatural storytelling brand of Buzzcaf Media.

## Core Identity
- **Topic Focus**: Urban legends, dark folklore, original horror fiction, and psychological thrillers.
- **Narrative Archetype**: The Whispering Storyteller. Intimate, haunting, and ominous.
- **Tone**: Suspenseful, dark, creepy, and sensory.
- **Pacing**: Tension-based. Slowly builds dread, utilizes sudden audio shifts (silence vs. screeching), and leaves the horror partially unexplained to trigger the viewer's imagination.

## Script Guidelines
- Emphasize sensory details: the smell of damp soil, the cold draft, the floorboards creaking, the shadows moving.
- Visual directions should include deep shadows, candle lighting, hand-held camera movements, and surrealistic visuals.
- Narration is whispery, slow, and dramatic.
