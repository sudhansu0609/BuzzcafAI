# Zunhindawt: Mizoram ka Khauf 💧

Mizoram ke pahado ki gehrayi mein ek aisa naam chupa hai, jise sunkar rounak nahi, sirf khauf mehsoos hota hai. (pause) Hum baat kar rahe hain 'Zunhindawt' ki. (normal)

Ye koi aam bhoot ya saaya nahi hai jo raaton mein darata hai. Zunhindawt ek aisa purana aur obscure entity hai jiska zikr Mizoram ke local folklore mein milta hai. Lekin iski kahani sunne ke baad aapka darr ka swaroop badal jayega. (pause) Kehte hain, ye makhlooq apni ek ajeeb aur behad ghinoni aadat ke liye jaana jata hai. (normal)

Zunhindawt... ye sirf ek naam nahi, balki ek darawni reality hai jo local kahaniyon mein saalon se dabi hui hai. Folklore ke mutabik, is creature ki sabse disturbing baat iska 'ritual' hai. (pause) Ye makhlooq zameen par bane un chhote puddles ya gaddhon mein apna munh dalta hai... jahan dusre logon ka peshab (urine) jama hota hai. (normal)

Sochiye, ek aisi cheez jo insani fitrat se bilkul bahar hai, wo in gandi jagahon ko apna shikar banati hai. Ye koi fictional horror nahi, balki Mizoram ke us purane folklore ka hissa hai jo batata hai ki kuch cheezein itni ghinoni aur ajeeb ho sakti hain ki unka tasavvur karna bhi mushkil hai. (pause) Kya aapne kabhi socha hai ki hamare aas-paas ki zameen ke niche aise rahasya chupe ho sakte hain? (beat) Kuch kahaniyan sirf darr nahi, balki us sach ka hissa hoti hain jise hum samajh nahi paate.
