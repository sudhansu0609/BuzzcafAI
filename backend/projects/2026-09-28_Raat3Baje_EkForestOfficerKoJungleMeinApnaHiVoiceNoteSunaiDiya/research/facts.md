# Factual Claims

- Acoustic Anomalies: Dense forest canopies and rock formations can create complex acoustic environments where sounds are trapped, reflected, and delayed, potentially creating a natural 'loop'.
- Auditory Pareidolia: The human brain's tendency to interpret random environmental noises (wind through leaves, cracking branches) as familiar patterns like human speech.
- Pishach/Pret Folklore: In various Indian regional traditions, these entities are known for mimicking voices or calling out names to lure victims away from safe paths into the deep woods.
- Electronic Interference: High-mineral content in certain forest soils and dense humidity can cause signal degradation and 'glitching' in digital audio files on mobile devices.
- Residual Haunting Theory: A paranormal concept where locations 'record' past events that play back under specific conditions, often cited in modern urban legends involving electronics.
