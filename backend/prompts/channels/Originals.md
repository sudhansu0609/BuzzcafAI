# Brand Style Guide: Originals

## Overview
Originals focuses on original fiction, poetry, books, and interactive storytelling.

## Core Identity
- **Topic Focus**: Creative stories, original poetry, slice-of-life vlogs, audiobooks, and thought-provoking literature.
- **Narrative Archetype**: The Café philosopher. Warm, introspective, creative, and relatable.
- **Tone**: Artistic, aesthetic, warm, nostalgic, and deep.
- **Pacing**: Relaxed, conversational, and reflective. Focuses on characters, inner thoughts, and aesthetic settings.

## Script Guidelines
- Focus heavily on character motivation and emotional arcs.
- Use warm color palettes, lo-fi background tracks, and soft light.
- Narration should feel close-mic, intimate, and friendly.

## Music
Follows the piece. Poetry and shayari: soft and intimate -- solo piano, santoor, sitar or sarangi, sufi or indian classical, calm/romantic/nostalgic, 60-75 bpm -- and `off` for the last couplet when it should land alone. Fiction: score the story's mood (romantic, mysterious, hopeful, sad) with 1-2 instruments; one change at the turn and one at the ending. Books and reviews: calm lo-fi or acoustic.
