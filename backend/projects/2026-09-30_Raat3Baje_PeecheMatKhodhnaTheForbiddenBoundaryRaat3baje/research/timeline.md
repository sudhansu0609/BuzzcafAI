# Timeline

- Ancient Era: Emergence of 'Gram Devtas' (Village Deities) as protectors of village boundaries and sacred land in Vedic traditions.
- Medieval Period: Integration of Tantric practices into rural folk-protection, leading to the development of 'Raksha Kavach' for sealing contested grounds.
- Pre-Modern Era: Formalization of 'Masaan' (cremation ground) boundaries where specific rituals were required to prevent spirit migration.
- 19th - Early 20th Century: Documentation of rural land disputes often involving 'haunted' claims as a means of maintaining traditional land sanctity.
- Present Day: Persistence of oral traditions regarding 'Peeche Mat Khodna' (Do not dig behind) in rural Indian construction.
