# Brand Style Guide: Life3Baje

## Essence (read first)
The creator's personal-brand DOCUMENTARY channel: his real, ongoing journey of building businesses, YouTube channels and eventually a media company (Buzzcaf Media, Buzzcaf Coffee) while keeping a full-time software career. Viewers follow a continuing story -- what he attempts, what actually happens, including failures -- not a set of unrelated videos.
- **Never** generic productivity, motivation, business advice or YouTube-growth content.
- **Pillars:** 1 building and running multiple YouTube channels; 2 turning them into a real media business; 3 revenue, costs, failures, experiments; 4 behind-the-scenes creation; 5 demonetisation, recovery, lessons; 6 building Buzzcaf and other businesses from scratch; 7 balancing a full-time software job; 8 monthly / 30-day progress reports with actual results; 9 experiments documented honestly; 10 major decisions, failures and changes of direction.
- **Tone:** honest, warm, conversational, first person; real numbers and real setbacks.
- **Language:** Hinglish in Roman script.
- **Shorts:** clips extracted from the long videos, plus the creator's recitations of Hindi poems and sher-o-shayari (classical to contemporary) -- recited verbatim, the poet always credited.

## Example topics
"I'm Trying to Build a Media Company While Working Full-Time" - "I Run 5 YouTube Channels -- Here's What It Actually Costs" - "My YouTube Channel Got Demonetized. What I Changed After That" - "Can I Build a Coffee Brand From Scratch in India?" - "30 Days of Building Buzzcaf -- Revenue, Mistakes, Reality".

## For every video idea develop
Core premise and viewer payoff; title options; thumbnail concept; opening hook; full Hinglish script when requested; A-roll setup; camera framing; scene-by-scene shot list; B-roll requirements; screen recordings where appropriate; locations/props; editing instructions; music/sound direction; Shorts/Reels to extract; connection to previous and future episodes.

## Continuity
Generate new topics from what the creator is actually building and what happened since the previous episodes (projects in the Studio, channel results, business milestones). Treat Life3Baje as one ongoing documentary.

## Music
Warm, understated instrumental beds that sit under an honest first-person story -- never trailer-epic, never hype. Openings and everyday building: lo-fi or acoustic, soft piano, acoustic guitar, bansuri flute, calm/hopeful, 80-95 bpm. Wins and real progress: hopeful or energetic, piano with light drums, 100-115 bpm. Failures, losses, demonetisation: sad solo violin or soft piano, slow, 65-75 bpm. A raw confession or a hard number: `off`. Indian colour (bansuri, santoor, tabla) is welcome; keep it gentle.
