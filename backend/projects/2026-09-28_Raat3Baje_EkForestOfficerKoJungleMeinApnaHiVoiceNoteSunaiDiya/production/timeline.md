# Sound Design Plan: Ek Forest Officer Ko Jungle Mein Apna Hi Voice Note Sunai Diya
**Brand:** Raat3Baje

To maintain the **Raat3Baje** aesthetic, the sound design must balance "grounded investigation" with "eerie folk-horror." The goal is to make the audience feel the humidity of the jungle and the psychological weight of the "time fold."

---

## 1. Global Audio Aesthetic
*   **Primary Texture:** **"Dense Wilderness Isolation."** Use heavy, low-frequency drones layered with organic forest textures (crickets, distant wind, rustling leaves). No high-pitched synth stings.
*   **The Silence Rule:** For every major reveal—specifically when the protagonist hears his own voice for the first time, identifies the "Time Fold," or mentions the logbook from 2016—the music must drop to **0% volume (Absolute Silence)** for **1.5–3 seconds** before the most chilling line is delivered.
*   **No Jump-Scare Rule:** No sudden "scary" noises. Tension must be built through frequency layering (low-end drones) and environmental textures.
*   **The Echo Effect:** When the voice note plays, apply a **Spatial Processing Effect** (Heavy Reverb + Delay) to make it sound like it is emerging from the trees/ground rather than just a radio.

## 3. Segment-Specific SFX Layering

### **Segment I: The Introduction (Sudhansu's Narration)**
*   **Atmosphere:** Low-volume forest ambience (crickets, rustling leaves).
*   **Key Note:** No music during the very first "Namaste" or intro; just a clean, grounded atmosphere.

### **Segment II: The Journey (The Jeep & No-Go Zone)**
*   **Vehicle SFX:** Low rumble of a heavy jeep engine on a muddy road (muffled).
*   **Environment:** Wet tires on leaves, the "thud" of mud being kicked up.
*   **Radio Static:** When Vikram checks his walkie-talkie, add a heavy **white noise/static burst**, then cut to silence when he says "Yahan se aage hum sirf apne upar nirbhar rahenge."

### **Segment III: The Echo & The Walkie-Talkie**
*   **The Thud:** A deep, organic thud (like wood hitting earth) every few seconds. It should feel heavy, not sharp.
*   **Voice Note Transition:** When the protagonist finds the walkie-talkie, add a **metallic clink** and a burst of static. 
*   **The Voice:** The "Officer's voice" must have a slight grain/distortion as if coming through old electronics, but it must be clearly his own voice (high clarity on words).

### **Segment IV: The Climax (The Logbook & Time Fold)**
*   **The Silence:** When the protagonist sees his name in 2016, **Drop to 0% volume.** Let the weight of that date sink in before he speaks.
*   **Final Sequence:** As he leaves the area, the "Echoes" should slowly fade into the background drone, leaving a lingering sense of dread.

## 4. Summary of Audio Cues for Editor
*   **Silence Points:** 1. Before "Beta, ye tumhari apni awaz hai." 2. Before "Mera naam wahan saaf dikh raha tha (2016)." 3. Just before the final closing line.
*   **Transition Style:** Cross-fades for all drone changes; no sudden cuts unless it is a deliberate "Silence Rule" moment.
*   **Vocal Processing:** Add slight reverb to the "Voice Note" portion to differentiate it from Sudhansu's direct narration.
