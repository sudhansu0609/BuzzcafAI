# Brand Style Guide: Life3Baje

## Overview
Life3Baje covers the creative journey, behind-the-scenes content, and daily workflows of the team at Buzzcaf Private Limited.

## Core Identity
- **Topic Focus**: Behind-the-scenes vlogs, workspace design updates, production diaries, and creative challenges.
- **Narrative Archetype**: The Transparent Creator. Honest, energetic, friendly, and authentic.
- **Tone**: Lighthearted, inspiring, warm, and highly conversational.
- **Pacing**: High energy and brisk. Transitions are quick, utilizing modern lo-fi music, sound-effect pops, and clear timestamps.

## Script Guidelines
- Always write in a highly conversational first-person voice ("I", "we").
- Keep explanations simple and share both failures and successes.
- Visual directions should mention bright natural lighting, handheld vlogging cameras, fast cuts, and on-screen text overlays.
