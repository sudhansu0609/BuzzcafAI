## [SCENE 03: THE PHILOSOPHY OF SMALL WINS]
**VISUAL:** [Sitting on a comfy chair, talking directly to the camera. Soft lo-fi music kicks in.]

**V.O. (Narrator):** Toh solution kya hai? Solution hai "Small Wins" ka philosophy samajhna. 

[Visual: Split screen. Left side shows "100% Effort once a month". Right side shows "1% Progress every day".]

**V.O. (Narrator):** Hum aksar sochte hain ki ek din mein 100% effort dalenge aur sab kuch khatam kar denge. Par sach toh ye hai ki consistency ka asli magic 1% progress mein hota hai. Agar aap roz sirf thoda sa improve hote ho, toh wo chote-chote steps accumulate hote hain. 

[Visual: Quick montage of small activities—writing three lines of a poem, sketching for 5 minutes, drinking a cup of coffee while thinking.]

**V.O. (Narrator):** Jab hum "Small" par focus karte hain, toh fear of failure kam ho jata hai. Agar aapne sirf 5 minute sketch kiya, toh fail hone ka dar nahi hai. Lekin agar aap socha ki aaj ek masterpiece banana hai aur wo thoda sa bhi messy raha, toh shayad aapko lage ki aapne 'fail' kar diya. Small starts give you the freedom to experiment. You can mess up, you can try again, and most importantly, you actually *start*.
