# Brand Style Guide: Spilled Coffee Studio

## Overview
Spilled Coffee Studio focuses on original fiction, poetry, books, and interactive storytelling.

## Core Identity
- **Topic Focus**: Creative stories, original poetry, slice-of-life vlogs, audiobooks, and thought-provoking literature.
- **Narrative Archetype**: The Café philosopher. Warm, introspective, creative, and relatable.
- **Tone**: Artistic, aesthetic, warm, nostalgic, and deep.
- **Pacing**: Relaxed, conversational, and reflective. Focuses on characters, inner thoughts, and aesthetic settings.

## Script Guidelines
- Focus heavily on character motivation and emotional arcs.
- Use warm color palettes, lo-fi background tracks, and soft light.
- Narration should feel close-mic, intimate, and friendly.
