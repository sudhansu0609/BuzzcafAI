# Night Shift ke Khaufnak Rules 🏢

Ek badi corporate building. Raat ke 2 baje. Poora floor khali hai, sirf aap aur aapka laptop hain. (pause) Lekin is office mein raat ki shift par kaam karne ke liye kuch aise rules hain, jinhe todne ka matlab hai... khud ko khatre mein daalna.

Ye ek kahani hai ek night-shift employee ki jise pehle din hi uske supervisor ne ek purani diary di. (normal) Usme likha tha: 'Agar yahan raat ko kaam karna hai, toh in 5 rules ka palan karein.'

Rule number ek: Agar corridor mein kisi ke chalne ki awaaz aaye, toh apni chair se hilein mat. 
Rule number do: Agar peeche se koi aapka naam pukaare, toh mud kar jawab na dein. 
Rule number teen: Agar lift ka darwaza apne aap khule, toh andar na jayein.
Rule number char: Agar 3 baje koi rone ki awaaz aaye, toh headphone laga kar baith jayein. (pause)

Aur Rule number paanch... agar koi darwaze par dastak de aur kahe 'Kya main andar aa sakta hoon?', toh bilkul khamosh rahein.

Ek raat, us employee ne ek galti kar di. (beat) Usne rule number chaar ignore kiya. Use laga ki shayad wo sirf ek prank hai. Jaise hi usne headphone utara, use mehsoos hua ki koi uske theek peeche khada hai... aur uski saanso ki thandak uske gardan par thi.

Agli subah, office ka cabin khula tha. Employee wahan nahi tha. Bas uski kursi par ek purana note rakha tha: 'Rule number paanch ko todne wale kabhi agle din tak nahi pahunchte.' (slow) Kya aap aaj raat ki shift ke liye taiyaar hain?
