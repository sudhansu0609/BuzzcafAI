# Namaskar doston, main hoon Shivraj aur aap sun rahe hain Hor

Namaskar doston, main hoon Shivraj aur aap sun rahe hain Horror Podcast Shivraj ke saath. Doston, aaj ki yeh kahani apne hi channel ke ek subscriber ke anubhav par aadharit hai. Yeh kahani us waqt ki hai jab wo forest officer ki naukri kiya karte the. Lekin kahani shuru karne se pehle aap sabhi logon se meri request hai ki agar aap channel par naye hain aur aap meri kahaniyon ko pasand karte hain toh mere channel ko zaroor subscribe karein. Toh chaliye ab bina waqt zaya kiye aaj ki kahani ko sunte hain. Kehte hain ki sunsaan gumnaam jagah par bhoot preton ka vaas hota hai. Ek waqt tak maine bhi kabhi bhoot pret in cheezon par yaqeen nahi kiya tha. Par ab karta hoon.

1999 ki wo daravani raat
Yeh un dinon ki baat hai jab main bataur forest officer naya-naya duty par join hua tha. Usi dauran shuruati dinon mein mere saath ek bahut hi daravana haadsa hua tha. Lekin bhagwan ka shukra hai ki main us haadse se sahi salaamat bach gaya tha. Yeh baat 1999 ki hai. December ka mahina tha. Meri pehli posting mere ghar se bahut door ki gayi thi. Duty par mujhe bas 2 mahine hue the. Wahi shanivar ka din tha aur usi din dopahar humein ek khabar mili ki jungle ke raste se jaanwaron ke sambandh mein kisi cheez ki taskari hone wali hai. Isliye main aur mere saath do vanrakshak hum teeno jungle ke aas-paas ke ilaake mein gasht laga rahe the. Main jungle ke paas se guzarne wali har ek cheez, har ek insaan par nazar rakhe hue tha. Mere saath jo do vanrakshak the unmein se ek ka naam Pawan aur doosre ka naam Rishi tha. Rishi aur Pawan ek alag jeep se toh main ek doosri jeep se us ilaake mein gasht laga rahe the. Jungle ke beech ek sunsaan jagah thi, jahan par van vibhag ki ek chhoti si chowki bani hui thi. Waise wahan koi rukta nahi tha. Kabhi koi afsar jungle ke andar jata toh wah suraksha aur aaram ke liye us jagah ka istemal kiya karta tha. Main toh pehli baar wahan gaya tha. Uss din pata nahi mujhe achanak se kya ho gaya tha. Shaam hote-hote meri tabiyat achanak se bigadti chali gayi. Upar se main theek lag raha tha. Par andar se bahut kamzori mehsoos ho rahi thi. Aisa lag raha tha jaise mujhe aaram karne ki sakht zaroorat hai. Main kuch der ke liye usi chowki par ruk gaya.

Rishi aur Pawan dono aas-paas ke ilaake mein gasht laga rahe the. Unhone mujhe ghar jaakar aaram karne ke liye kaha. Lekin main unki baat maane ko tayar nahi tha. Maine unhe kaha ki agar wahan kuch bhi galat hua toh woh meri zimmedari hogi. Isliye kisi bhi haalat mein main wahan se nahi jaunga. Ulta unke baar-baar kehne par mujhe un par hi shak hone laga aur isliye maine wahin chowki par rukne ka nirnay liya. Maine unhe kaha ki main yahin rukunga. Par jab maine yeh kaha toh Pawan aankhein badi-badi kar mujhe ghoorne laga. Maine uska chehra dekh usse poochha, kya hua? Tum aise kyun dekh rahe ho? Pawan ne apni nazar hatate hue kaha, "Sar, koi bhi afsar yahan raat mein is chowki par nahi rukta hai."

Maine halka sa muskurate hue use jawab diya. Haan, par meri tabiyat kharab hai aur main nahi chahta ki meri ghair haaziri mein yahan koi bhi gairkanuni kaam ho. Rishi ne aage aate hue kaha, "Sar, yeh jungle raat mein rukne ke liye accha nahi hai. Khaas karke yeh jagah chahein toh hum paas ke gaanv mein jaakar ruk sakte hain."

Mujhe un dono ki baatein sunkar un par aur shak ho raha tha. Laga ki shayad yeh dono bhi taskari mein shamil hain. Toh aakhirkar maine unhe aadesh diya ki tum dono jaakar mere khane ka bandobast karo aur ho sake toh dawaiyon ka bhi. Par main yahan se kahin nahi jaane wala. Mera aadesh sunkar Pawan phir se aage badha. "Saab aap pehli baar is chowki par aaye ho. Hum yahan pichle 5 saalon se hain. Is jungle ko aap nahi jaante. Yahan is jungle mein sach mein khatra hai."

Maine Pawan ke kandhe par haath rakhte hue kaha, waise toh insaano ko har ek jungle se khatra hai par hum vanrakshak hain aur humein isi baat ka pagar milta hai. Rishi ne meri baat ko beech mein todte hue kaha, "Sar, aap hamari baat ko samajh nahi rahe. Yahan aapko kisi jangli jaanwar se khatra nahi hai balki bhoot pret se hai."

Uski woh baat sunkar maine gusse mein hi use kaha, "Pagalon jaisi baat mat karo. Yeh sab bani banayi baatein hoti hain."

Rishi ne phir mujhe samjhane ki koshish ki. "Sar, bahut logon ne yahan ek bhoot ko dekha hai aur sirf logon ne hi nahi, hamare kuch afsaron ne bhi yahan kuch ajeeb sa mehsoos kiya hai. Kehte hain ki yahan andhere mein ek pretaatma ghumti rehti hai aur woh logon ka shikaar karti hai."

Un dono ki baatein sunkar mera gussa aur bhi badhta chala gaya. Maine ek aakhri baar un dono se poochha kya tumne dekha hai us pretaatma ko? Un dono ne na mein apna sar hilaya. Toh phir theek hai. Tum dono bhi mere saath ab yahin rukenge. Jab tak hum us aatma ko dekh nahi lete hum yahan se kahin nahi jayenge.

Meri baat sunkar dono bhi ghabra gaye aur us baat ko talne lage. Ghar jaane ka bahana banane lage. Toh maine unhe samjhate hue kaha, "Dekho baat ko samjho. Yeh sab yahan ke gunde badmash logon ne failayi hui baatein hoti hain. Yeh sab afwahayein hain. Woh log yehi chahte hain ki hum log unke ilaake se door rahein. Un logon ko galat kaam karne ke liye aisi baatein badhava deti hain aur isliye yeh log aas-paas ke gaanv mein logon mein aisi afwahayein failate hain. Tum dono agar nahi rukna chahte ho toh na roko lekin maine jo kaha hai utna kaam karo."

Meri baat ko sunkar dono chup ho gaye aur aapas mein kuch phusphusate hue chowki se bahar chale gaye. Kuch der baad mujhe gaadi ki aawaz sunai di. Matlab aakhirkar woh dono bhi wahan se chale gaye the. Maine chowki ko andar se theek se dekha. Wahan kuch daru ki botalein, kuch gilaas dhool mein pade hue the. Kuch saaman tha lekin gair zaroori tha. Chowki ke ek deewar par jungle ka naksha bana hua tha. Maine us nakshe ke paas jaakar us chowki ko dhoondha. Nakshe mein us chowki ke peeche ek neeli rekha khinchi hui thi. Matlab chowki ke peeche se zaroor koi nadi guzarti hogi. Maine chowki ke peeche ki khidhki khol kar dekha. Par peeche ka ilaaka ghane pedon aur jhaadiyon se bhara hua tha.

Andhera hone mein abhi waqt tha. Toh main chowki ke bahar chala gaya. Jaise-jaise shaam dhal rahi thi, aas-paas thand bhi badhne lagi thi. Main chowki ke peeche ki taraf chala gaya. Peeche ka ilaaka bahut hi ghana tha. Woh ilaaka jitna ghana tha, utna hi wahan sannata tha. Door kahin se jangli jaanwaron ki aawazein aa rahi thi. Main wahan se behti nadi ko dekhna chahta tha. Main un oonche pedon aur jhaadiyon ke beech se aage chala gaya. Yeh baat sach thi ki main pehli baar wahan aaya tha aur isliye main us jagah ke baare mein bilkul bhi jaanta nahi tha. Kuch 220 meter aage jaane ke baad mujhe thodi dhalan dikhayi di aur usi dhalan se aage ek nadi beh rahi thi. Main us dhalan se utar kar neeche chala gaya. Nadi ka paani ekdam shaant tha. Nadi ke paas pedon mein reh rahe panchiyon ki chahchahat ki aawazein aa rahi thi. Sare panchi wapas apne ghar laut rahe the. Matlab andhera hone mein ab kuch hi waqt baaki tha. Tab mujhe yaad aaya ki main torch lana toh bhool hi gaya tha. Aas-paas andhera bhi hone laga tha. Isliye main zyada der wahan nahi ruka aur wapas apni chowki par chala gaya. Chowki ke andar jaate hi maine darwaza band kar liya aur sirf peeche ki khidhki ko khula chhod diya. Ab halka-halka badan dard bhi hone laga tha. Maine wahin ek kursi ko saaf kiya aur us kursi ko lekar khidhki ki taraf peeth karke baith gaya. Peeche ki thandi hawa ke jhonke khidhki se andar aa rahe the. Baithe-baithe main apne haathon se apni hi gardan aur sar ko malish kar raha tha. Tabhi mujhe aisa laga jaise khidhki ke baare mere peeche koi khada hai. Main apni halchal rok kar dhyan se peeche se aa rahi aahat ko sunne ki koshish karne laga.

Pehli baar kuch mehsoos hua
Aisa laga jaise mere peeche khade hokar koi zor-zor se saans le raha ho. Itna hi nahi main un saanson ki garmi ko bhi apni gardan par mehsoos kar pa raha tha. Maine dheere se apni bandook ko haath mein liya aur jhat se peeche mudkar dekha. Par wahan toh kuch bhi nahi tha. Maine khidhki se bahar andhere mein dekhne ki koshish ki. Par wahan door-door tak kuch bhi dikhayi nahi diya. Wahan zyada andhera toh nahi hua tha. Par sooraj lagbhag doob chuka tha. Ek tarah ka halka andhera us roshni mein har taraf phail gaya tha. Main wahan se apni nazar hatata ki tabhi maine door pedon ke beech kisi insaan jaisi aakriti ko sarakte hue dekha.

Maine wahin padi torch uthai aur us dhundhle andhere mein door dekhne ki koshish ki. Par ab wahan sab kuch normal tha. Phir bhi main kaafi der tak us taraf dekhta raha, ki kahin koi halchal nazar aa jaye. Lekin us halchal ke baad wahan kuch bhi nahi hua. Phir socha ki yeh jungle hai toh aisi aahat, aisi aawazein aana aam baat hai. Itne se mujhe darne ki koi zaroorat nahi. Is baat ko sochkar main khidhki ki taraf mooh karke baith gaya. Tabhi mujhe bahar se gaadi ki aawaz sunai padi. Kuch 1 ghante baad Pawan aur Rishi wapas aa gaye the. Unke aane tak wahan sab kuch andhere mein doob chuka tha. Toh main unhe phir kaha rukna chahte ho toh ruk sakte hain. Rishi ne apni gardan na mein hilate hue jawab diya. "Saab abhi waqt hai. Hamesha vish ki pariksha lena accha nahi hota. Hum paas ke gaanv mein jaakar ruk sakte hain aur wahan se bhi dhyan rakh sakte hain."

Maine unki baat ko saaf mana kar diya aur wahin par rukne ki apni ichcha zahir ki. "Accha theek hai. Main apna khayal rakhunga. Par tum bhi aas-paas ki harkaton par nazar rakhna. Kuch bhi galat lage toh seedha mujhe yahan aa kar report karna."

Meri baat sunkar woh dono bhi wahan se chale gaye. Maine darwaza band kar liya aur wahin table par apna khana khatam kiya. Wahan jo bijli aa rahi thi, woh paas ke gaanv se aa rahi thi. 8 baje ke kareeb bijli achanak se chali gayi. Maine andhere mein tatol kar dekha. Pata nahi ab yeh bijli wapas aayegi bhi ya nahi. Mujhe kuch doori par koi halchal si mehsoos hui. Woh sookhe patton ki sarsarahat ki aawaz thi. Andhere mein wahan kuch chamak raha tha. Jaise koi door khade hokar us ghane andhere mein mujhe ghoor raha ho. Jaise hi main ek kadam aage badha, usi ke saath-saath mujhe patton ki aawaz sunai di. Jaise mere aage badhne ke saath hi kisi aur ne bhi apna kadam aage badhaya ho. Meri bandook chowki ke andar thi. Khidki ke bahar wo ladki

Nadi ke paas ka aakhri samna

Maine khidhki ko poora khol diya aur apni suraksha ke liye deewar se satkar khada ho gaya. Abhi bhi woh aawaz mujhe aa rahi thi. Koi chal raha tha aur saath hi kisi cheez ko ghasit raha tha. Ab dono taraf ghana andhera tha. Main apne ek haath mein torch lekar aur doosre haath mein bandook lekar jaakar khidhki ke samne khada ho gaya. Bahar koi hota bhi toh bhi mujhe dekh nahi pata.

Maine us aawaz ki disha mein bandook ko samne ki taraf taante hue torch ko jhat se on kiya. Wahan paas mein toh koi nahi tha. Par khidhki se kaafi door koi chal raha tha.

Khidki ke bahar wo ladki
Woh koi ladki thi shayad. Par woh ladki ek pair se langdate hue apne doosre pair ko ghasit-te hue chal rahi thi. Uske badan par poore kapde nahi the. Kamar par bas ek phata hua kapde ka tukda latak raha tha. Main thoda ghabraya zaroor tha. Par woh dekhkar main yeh sochne laga ki itni raat ko koi jawan ladki aise ghane andhere mein yahan kya kar rahi hogi? Kya woh is jungle ko jaanti hai? Kya woh yahin rehti hai ya kahin woh rasta bhatak gayi hai. Ho sake shayad woh yahin ki koi aadiwasi ladki ho. Jungle ek aisi jagah hoti hai jahan aap kitni bhi himmat karo kitna bhi hausla rakho lekin dar lagta zaroor hai. Maine torch ki roshni mein khidhki se bahar aas-paas nazar ghumakar dekha. Pedon ke upar jhaadiyon ke beech har taraf dekhkar maine tasalli kar li. Wahan koi insaan koi jaanwar nahi tha. Woh ladki akeli hi nadi ki taraf badhe ja rahi thi. Torch ki roshni us tak pahunch rahi thi. Par phir bhi usne ek baar bhi mudkar meri taraf nahi dekha. Main yeh soch raha tha ki aakhir yeh ladki karna kya chahti hai? Main torch ko band kar wahin par khada hokar yeh sochne laga ki main aage kya karoon? Bahar jaoon ya nahi? Woh ladki yahan tak kaise aayi hogi? Upar se woh apaahij hai. Kisi jangli jaanwar ne us par hamla kar diya toh woh kuch nahi kar payegi. Zaroor uske saath koi haadsa hua hoga aur shayad woh kisi sadme mein hogi. Par woh nadi ki taraf kyun ja rahi hai? Aise kai sawaal us waqt mere man mein uth rahe the.

Aakhirkar mujhse raha nahi gaya. Main apni torch aur bandook lekar seedhe chowki ke bahar chala gaya. Maine apne upar kambal odh rakha tha taaki main kambal mein apni bandook ko chhupa sakoon. Main torch ki roshni mein us ghane andhere ke beech chowki ke peeche chala gaya. Mere wahan jaane tak woh ladki kaafi door nikal gayi thi. Isliye main us disha mein thoda tez chalne laga. Us mahaul mein maine use aawaz dena theek nahi samjha. Kyunki jangli ilaake mein jaanwar hamesha taak mein rehte hain. Chalte-chalte main dhalan ke kareeb pahunch gaya. Lekin us dhalan se utar kar woh kahan chali gayi thi mujhe pata nahi tha. Main dhalan se utar kar nadi ke paas pahunch gaya. Nadi ke aas-paas bhi koi nahi tha. Woh ladki bhi nahi. Mere wahan aane se pehle woh kahan gayab ho gayi thi pata hi nahi chal. Aisa lag raha tha mano wahan ka andhera use nigal gaya ho. Maine jitna ho sakta tha utna jagah par use chhaan mara. Par uska kahin koi pata nahi chal. Us ladki ko dhoondhte-dhoondhte mera ek pair keechad mein dhans gaya. Maine dekha ki us keechad mein chalne ke aur ghasitne ke pairon ke nishaan the. Main samajh gaya ki ladki yahin kahin hai. Usi waqt mujhe nadi ke paani mein bulbulon ki aawaz sunai padi. Paani ke upar lagataar bulbule ban rahe the. Socha kahin yeh ladki paani mein toh nahi gayi. Main daudkar nadi ke paani ke paas chala gaya. Torch ki roshni mein paani ke andar jhaank kar dekhne laga. Woh paani bahut shaant tha. Kahin koi halchal nahi thi. Koi aawaz nahi thi. Paani ke andar sab kuch dhundhla-dhundhla sa dikhayi de raha tha. Main gaur se paani ke andar dekh raha tha. Tabhi koi cheez paani ke andar se chamakne lagi. Do binduon jaisi. Maine un binduon ko aur zyada dhyan se dekhne ki koshish ki. Woh shayad kisi ki chamakti hui aankhein thi. Main ekdam shaant tha. Bina apni palkein jhapkaaye main un aankhon ko dekhne laga. Mera shareer tab ekdam thanda pad chuka tha. Woh aankhein mujhe paani ke andar bula rahe the. Pata nahi main kitni der se wahan par baitha tha.

Nadi ke paas ka aakhri samna
Maine dekha ki dheere-dheere paani ke andar se do haath meri taraf badh rahe the. Meri dhadkan kaafi tez chal rahi thi. Shayad kisi anjaan dar se main andar se dara hua tha. Tab mujhe aisa lagne laga tha ki main koi shikaar hoon, jo kisi shikaari ke jaal mein fashta ja raha hai.

Woh haath paani se nikal kar bahar aa gaye aur dheere se meri gardan ko pakadte hue peeche ki taraf chale gaye. Woh dono haath meri gardan ko kaskar pakade hue the. Un haathon ke geele chipchipe sparsh se mera poora badan sehar utha. Ek anjaan dar ki lehar mere poore shareer mein daud gayi. Main kuch samajh paata tabhi paani ke andar ek anjaan chehra bilkul mere chehre ke samne ubharne laga. Bahut hi ajeeb aur safed chehra. Upar aate-aate uska mooh khulta ja raha tha. Uske saare daant nukile the. Aankhein chamak rahi thi. Aisa lag raha tha jaise koi jaanwar apna jabda khol raha ho. Itna bada ki uske andar mera poora sar sama jaye. Mujhe apne shareer par zara bhi kaaboo nahi tha. Mera shareer kisi machine ki tarah thartharane laga. Sab kuch dekhte hue bhi mujh mein apne aap ko chhudaane ki taakat nahi thi. Usi pal un dono haathon ke nakhun peeche se meri gardan mein ghus gaye. Ek tez dard ki lehar gardan se ubhar kar dimaag tak chali gayi. Us dard se mujhe ek zor ka jhatka sa laga aur usi ke saath meri shirt mein atki hui gale ki ek maala chhootkar us ladki ke aankhon ke samne latakne lagi.

Maala ko dekh us ladki ne jhatke se mujhe door kar diya. Woh ek rudraksh ki maala thi. Us jhatke se main peeche keechad mein ja gira. Woh ladki kisi magarmach jaise paani ki satah par aa gayi aur mujhe dekh woh ghurane lagi. Jaise woh mujh par hamla karna chahti ho. Is poore maamle mein meri bandook kahan gayi thi mujhe pata nahi chal. Torch jal rahi thi isliye main use dekh pa raha tha. Main ladkhadata hue torch ko uthaya aur uski roshni ko us ladki ke chehre par mara. Dikhne mein woh ek sundar ladki thi par uske badan par kapde nahi the. Uska mooh kisi khoonkhar jaanwar jaise khula tha. Aankhein andhere mein chamak rahi thi. Bade-bade nukile mude hue nakhun the. Woh bhayanak roop dekhkar main wahan se peeche hatne laga. Badan chipchipa tha aur shareer par har jagah par chhilne ke aur katne ke nishaan the. Woh langdate hue meri taraf badhne lagi. Main uski chaal ko dekhkar samajh gaya ki yeh wahi ladki hai jise maine khidhki ke baahar dekha tha. Main wahan se daudta hua chowki ki taraf chala gaya. Chowki ke andar jaate hi maine darwaza laga liya. Saari khidhkiyan band kar li aur dar ke maare main wahin ek kone mein baith gaya. Tab main yeh soch raha tha ki jise maine dekha tha woh kya tha? Kaun thi woh ladki? Woh sach mein koi ladki thi ya kuch aur tha? Mujhe woh kyun marna chahti thi? Main wahan par baithe-baithe dar aur thand se kaamp raha tha. Kaafi der baad mujhe gaadi ki aawaz sunai di. Maine darwaze ke daraar se bahar jhaank kar dekha. Dekha toh Pawan aur Rishi jeep lekar wahan aa gaye the. Maine usi haalat mein darwaza khola. Meri haalat dekh dono dar gaye. Mere gardan se khoon beh raha tha. Aankhein laal thi, shareer tharthar kaamp raha tha aur mujhe bahut tez bukhar tha. Rishi ne mere haath ko pakad kar mujhe uthate hue kaha. "Sar, sar aapko aapko toh bahut tez bukhar hai aur yeh gardan se khoon kaisa?"

Khoon dekhkar Pawan bhi tezi se aage badha aur doosri taraf se mujhe sambhalne laga. Meri haalat ko dekh woh mujhe kosne laga. "Maine kaha tha na sar aapse ki yahan मत rukna par aap kaha mante ho? Accha hua ki bade saab ne humein aapko wapas laane ko kaha. Isliye hum dono wapas aa gaye. Warna na jaane yahan kya se kya ho jata."

Main un dono se kuch bhi bolne ki haalat mein nahi tha. Maine bas unhe jaldi se aspataal jaane ka ishara kiya. Us waqt aur uske kuch dinon baad bhi maine kisi ko kuch nahi bataya ki mere saath wahan kya hua tha. Maine aspataal mein bas itna kaha ki kisi jangli jaanwar ne khidhki se mere upar hamla kar diya tha aur yeh usi ke nakhun ke nishaan hain. Aspataal se theek hone ke baad main phir se us jagah par chhaanbeen karne chala gaya. Aas-paas ke rehne wale gaanv walon se logon se us jagah ke baare mein poochhtaach ki.

Sachayi ka khulasa
Tab mujhe pata chal ki kuch saal pehle us jagah par kuch darindon ne milkar ek ladki ke sath durvyavhaar kiya tha. Ek sundar ladki ko apni vaasna ka shikaar banaya tha. Apni vaasna ko poora karne ke baad un logon ne us ladki ko zakhmi halat mein us nadi mein phenk diya tha aur unhi logon ne gaanv mein yeh faila diya tha ki woh ladki kisi ke saath bhaag gayi hai. Kuch dinon baad logon ko bhi yahi laga ki woh sach mein kahin bhaag gayi hai. Par aas-paas ke gaanv ke logon ne kuch dinon baad use nadi ke paas ghumte hue dekha. Jab woh use dekhne nadi ke paas chale gaye toh unhe wahan nadi ke paani mein us ladki ki laash mili aur tab jaakar us ladki ke saath hue haadse ke baare mein logon ko pata chal aur yeh bhi ki jise unhone dekha tha woh us ladki ki aatma thi kyunki laash milne ke baad post-mortem se yeh pata laga tha ki us ladki ki maut hue 10 din guzar gaye the aur tab se ab tak gaanv ke kai logon ne use nadi ke aas-paas ghumte hue dekha tha. Doston, zaroori nahi ki jis baat par tum yaqeen nahi karte, woh saari baatein jhooth ho. Kabhi-kabhi doosron ki baaton par bhi yaqeen kar lena chahiye.
