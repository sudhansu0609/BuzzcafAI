# Research Document: The Spilled Coffee Mystery

## Overview
This document compiles comprehensive historical research regarding the origins of coffee houses and their connection to secret societies, literary clubs, and revolutionary ideas of the 18th century.

## Key Findings
1. **The Catalyst of Intellect**: Coffee houses in London, often called "Penny Universities," became critical meeting places for artists, politicians, and writers.
2. **The Social Leveler**: Unlike taverns, coffee houses encouraged sober debate and intellectual discussions, regardless of social status.
3. **The Spilled Coffee Phenomenon**: Many famous works of fiction were outlined on napkins stained with spilled coffee.
