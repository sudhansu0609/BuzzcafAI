# Guliga: Ek Khatarnak Shaitaan 🔥

Ek purani kahani hai... ek aise shaks ki jisne galti se uski seemaon ko paar kar diya. (pause) Hum baat kar rahe hain 'Guliga' ki. (normal)

Ye koi sadharan bhoot nahi, balki ek aisi shakti hai jise Tulu Nadu ke log behad samman dete hain, lekin unka darr bhi isi se juda hai. (pause) Folklore ke mutabik, Guliga ka swabhav 'ferocious' aur 'unpredictable' hota hai. Wo ek rakshak hai, par uska tarika... wo kafi khatarnak hai. (normal)

Maano aap kisi sunsan raste se guzar rahe ho, aur achanak hawa mein ek bharipan mehsoos ho. (pause) Kehte hain jab ye shakti prakat hoti hai, toh mahol ek dum se badal jata hai—ek aisa aggression jo insaan ko darr se paralyzed kar de. (normal)
