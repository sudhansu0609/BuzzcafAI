# Brand Style Guide: Khayal3Baje

## Overview
Khayal3Baje is the premium horror, folklore, and supernatural storytelling brand of Buzzcaf Media.

## Core Identity
- **Topic Focus**: Urban legends, dark folklore, original horror fiction, and psychological thrillers.
- **Narrative Archetype**: The Whispering Storyteller. Intimate, haunting, and ominous.
- **Tone**: Suspenseful, dark, creepy, and sensory.
- **Pacing**: Tension-based. Slowly builds dread, lets silence and stillness do the work, and leaves the horror partially unexplained to trigger the viewer's imagination.

## Script Guidelines
- Emphasize sensory details: the smell of damp soil, the cold draft, the floorboards creaking, the shadows moving.
- Visual directions should include deep shadows, candle lighting, slow steady camera movement, and quietly surreal visuals.
- Narration is whispery, slow, and dramatic.

## Edit and visuals (story style -- the creator's rule, 2026-10-02)
- Pictures and videos only. No subtitles/captions, headings, chapter titles, title cards, text on screen, newspaper or case-file cutaways, maps, charts, zoom punches, flashes, shakes or glitch hits. The render turns them off and strips them; do not plan them.
- No whooshes or sound effects. The music bed stays, far under the voice. The creator adds any sound edit by hand.
- About half of the visuals are moving clips (the creator can raise it to 100% per project). Clips go where something moves: a door easing open, a figure walking away, fog over the road.
- The visuals tell the story in order, like a storyboard: each shot is the next moment of what is being narrated, in the same place and the same night, until the story moves on. No generic, stock or news-looking pictures.
- Same characters in every shot. Each character has one fixed look (age, face, hair, clothes) from the project's cast sheet (`production/cast_sheet.json`), repeated word for word in every image and video prompt.
- Calm and scary, never rushed or news-like: slow steady camera, long quiet shots, darkness, mist and stillness carry the dread.

## Music
Cinematic horror storytelling: horror_dark under the telling (eerie drones, low strings, dissonant piano, tanpura drone), rising suspense as the story tightens, horror_climax (taiko drums, brass, aggressive strings) at the climax, and a soft, sad or eerie ending. Folk colour -- sarangi, shehnai, dholak -- for village and period stories. `off` for a whispered line or the moment before the reveal.
