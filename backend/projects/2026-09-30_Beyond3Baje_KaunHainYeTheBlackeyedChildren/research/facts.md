# Factual Claims

- Physical Description: Reported entities consistently have eyes that appear entirely black, with no visible iris or sclera.
- Common Behavior Patterns: Early reports from the 1980s/90s emphasize a specific behavior: children asking for permission to enter a home or car (e.g., 'Can we come in?' or 'Where is your mother?').
- Physiological Response: Many witnesses report an immediate, instinctive feeling of dread or a biological fear response before the interaction begins.
- Geographic Distribution: Documented reports prior to 2010 are concentrated in North America (USA, Canada), but similar accounts exist in Europe and Australia.
- Distinction between Report vs. Fiction: Historical reports focus on 'unsettling appearance' and 'strange requests,' while modern creepypasta adds elements like 'glitching,' supernatural abilities, and specific ritualistic behaviors.
