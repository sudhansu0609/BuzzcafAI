# Factual Claims

- The location is Kholat Syakhl (Mountain of the Dead), a remote, high-altitude pass in the Ural Mountains.
- The tent was found with several cuts on the exterior fabric, which forensic analysis suggests were made from the inside.
- Several victims were discovered partially dressed (some in underwear or socks), indicating an immediate and desperate flight into sub-zero temperatures.
- Medical reports confirmed internal injuries, including fractured ribs and skull fractures, in some victims without corresponding external bruising.
- Footprints leading away from the tent showed evidence of rapid movement, with varying sizes and types appearing to indicate different pairs of shoes or barefooted individuals.
- Initial Soviet records reported 'anomalous' radiation levels on certain items found within the camp (notably personal belongings).
- The area is characterized by extreme weather patterns and topographical features capable of producing infrasound under specific wind conditions.
