# Railway Track ke Raaz: Railroad Wraiths 🚂

Train ki patriyan... ek aisa raasta jo aksar sunsaan aur khaufnaak hota hai. (pause) Bengal Nagpur Railway se jude kuch purane kisse batate hain ki ye tracks sirf lohe ke tukde nahi, balki kai purani yaadon aur 'railroad wraiths' ka ghar hain. (normal)

Ye koi sirf kahani nahi hai. Purane zamane ke railway employees ne khud in 'wraiths' ke baare mein kafi warnings share ki hain. Kehte hain... (pause) ye wo aatmaein hain jo raste par bhatakti rehti hain, unka intezar karte hain jo kabhi pahunch hi nahi paate.

Ek sachhi ghatna ek driver ne sunayi thi—ek sunsaan jungle ke raaste par raat ka waqt tha. Ek joda (couple) lift maang raha tha. Driver ne pehle toh ignore kiya, par phir dil ki sunkar peeche muda aur unhe pick kiya. (normal)

Lekin jaise hi usne car ko thoda aage badhaya aur headlights on ki... wo dono gayab the! (pause) Na koi insaan dikha, na koi gaadi. Sirah ka baal khada ho gaya kyunki pata tha ki unka wahan hona hi namumkin tha. (beat)

Raat ke sannate mein jab train ki seeti sunayi deti hai... toh yaad rakhiye, har saaya insaan nahi hota. (slow) Kuch raaste sirf aapke liye nahi hote. Kya aapne kabhi raat ko kisi sunsaan track par kuch ajeeb mehsoos kiya hai?
