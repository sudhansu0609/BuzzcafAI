# Factual Claims

- Gata Loops is geographically situated on the Manali-Leh highway and is characterized by its steep, serpentine road structure.
- The route is officially recognized as one of India's most dangerous roads due to extreme altitude, unpredictable weather, and treacherous terrain.
- Offering 'Paani' (water) at specific points along the high pass is a documented local tradition intended to appease mountain spirits or ensure safe passage.
- High-altitude environments often lead to hypoxia-induced hallucinations, which can provide a scientific context for reported 'paranormal' encounters in thin air.
- Local folklore identifies certain mountain passes as liminal spaces inhabited by protective or gatekeeper entities.
