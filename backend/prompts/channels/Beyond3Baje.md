# Brand Style Guide: Beyond3Baje

## Overview
Beyond3Baje is the flagship intellectual documentary brand of Buzzcaf Media.

## Core Identity
- **Topic Focus**: Unsolved mysteries, historical anomalies, deep-dive investigations, and science explorations.
- **Narrative Archetype**: The Scholarly Detective. Objective, curious, intellectual, and deeply analytical.
- **Tone**: Cinematic, thought-provoking, serious, and deeply engaging.
- **Pacing**: Deliberate and suspenseful. Starts with an unresolved paradox, builds evidence, presents hypotheses, and concludes with a philosophical takeaway.

## Script Guidelines
- Avoid sensationalist exaggerations. Maintain credibility.
- Use macro shots, historical sketches, and moody atmospheric descriptions.
- Voiceover should sound deep, calm, and well-paced.
