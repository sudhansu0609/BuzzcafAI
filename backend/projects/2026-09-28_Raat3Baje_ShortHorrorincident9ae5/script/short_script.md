# NH-17 ka Khaufnak Sach 💀

NH-17... yaani aaj ka NH 66. Mumbai se Goa jaante hue, ye rasta jitna sundar dikhta hai, raat ke sannate mein utna hi khaufnaak ban jata hai. (pause) Log kehte hain ki is raste par kuch aisa hai jo sirf andhere mein bahar aata hai. Ek couple ka kissa aaj bhi logon ki rooh kaanp deta hai. Wo Goa ja rahe the... aur raat ke sannate mein, ek sunsan mod par unhe laga jaise koi peeche baitha hai. (pause) Gaadi ki lights mein unhone dekha—ek dhundli si parchhayi jo raaste ke kinare khadi thi. Jab unhone break maara, toh wo cheez gayab ho gayi, lekin gaadi ke andar ek ajeeb si thandak aur purani mitti ki mehak reh gayi. (beat) Kehte hain ye sirf ek kahani nahi, balki un hazaron drivers ki warning hai jo raat ko is sunsaan raste par nikalte hain. Kuch log ise bhoot ka kissa maante hain, toh kuch kehte hain ki ye lambe safar aur akelapan ka darr hai... (slow) Lekin jab aap us khali sadak par honge aur peeche se koi awaaz aayegi, toh aapko fark nahi padega ki wo sach hai ya sirf ek kahani. Kya aap himmat karenge NH-17 par raat ko drive karne ki?
