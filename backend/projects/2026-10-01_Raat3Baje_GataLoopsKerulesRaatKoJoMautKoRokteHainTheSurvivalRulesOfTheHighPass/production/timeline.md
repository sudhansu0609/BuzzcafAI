# Sound Design Plan: Gata Loops ke 'Rules' | The Survival Rules of the High Pass

This plan bridges the gap between cinematic horror and grounded investigation, ensuring that the atmosphere of the high-altitude Himalayan pass feels suffocating and authentic.

---

## 1. Global Audio Aesthetic
*   **Primary Texture:** **"High-Altitude Isolation."** A blend of heavy wind textures, low-frequency sub-bass drones, and "hollow" acoustic elements (Tanpura/Sarangi). 
*   **The Silence Rule:** For the 12 Rules in Section 3, a **hard cut to 0% volume** for 1.5–2 seconds must occur immediately before each rule is read and after the final word of the rule is spoken. This emphasizes the "weight" of the warning.
*   **No Jump-Scare Rule:** No high-frequency synth stings or sudden "scary" noises. Tension must be built through frequency layering (low-end drones) and environmental textures.

## 3. Layered SFX & Environmental Textures

### **A. The Atmosphere (Environment)**
*   **Himalayan Wind:** A "howling" wind texture that feels like it’s blowing through rocks, not just open space. Use this as a constant base in the early scenes.
*   **The Muffle Effect:** When the fog is described as "thick," apply a low-pass filter to all ambient sounds (truck, wind) to create a claustrophobic sensation.
*   **Distorted Radio:** For Rule 10, use heavy white noise and crackling static that cuts in and out of the audio.

### **B. The Narrative Elements (Action)**
*   **Metal-on-Metal:** A heavy "thud" and a metallic grinding sound for the truck's exterior when something is mentioned on the roof or door.
*   **Paper Textures:** For the "Survival Manual," use high-quality foley of thick, dry paper being flipped/crinkled to emphasize its age.
*   **The Door Creak:** A slow, heavy, rusted iron gate/door creaking sound for the old man’s cabin.

### **C. Specific Cue Triggers (Script Integration)**
| Script Trigger | Action | Timing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"Piche mat dekhna!"* | **Distortion Overlay** - Add a heavy, metallic reverb/delay to the voice. | Instant |
| **Rule 1-12** | **The Silence Cut** - Drop all music and background noise to absolute zero. | Before & After each rule |
| *"Raste par koi bachcha..."* | **Distant Cry** - A very faint, high-frequency crying sound buried deep in the mix (almost barely audible). | Under V.O. |
| *Section 4 Transition* | **The "Reality" Ping** - A subtle metallic chime or a low-pass filter sweep to indicate the host is taking over. | At transition point |

## 4. Production Notes for Editor
1.  **Spatial Audio:** In Section 2, pan the sound of the wind and the truck's engine slightly to create a sense of space before it "muffles" in the fog.
2.  **The Rule Pulse:** During the "Rules" segment (Section 3), the drone should subtly increase in volume/intensity as we approach Rule 10, then drop back down for the final warning.
3.  **Tail Ends:** Ensure that after a "Silence Cut," any reverb tail from the previous line is cut cleanly to ensure the silence feels absolute.
