# Night Guard ka Khaufnak Sach 🛡️

Ek purani building... aur ek night-shift duty. (pause) Log sochte hain ki security guards ka kaam sirf darr se ladna hai, par asli darr tab hota hai jab aapko pata chale ki aap akele nahi hain. 

Ye ek kahani hai ek guard ki jise ek purane warehouse mein duty di gayi thi. Use ek diary mili, jisme likha tha: 'Agar yahan raat ko rehna hai, toh in 5 rules ka palan karein.' (normal)

Rule number ek: Agar koi bahar se darwaza khatkhataye aur kahe ki wo 'delivery' dene aaya hai, toh bahar mat nikalna. Wo sirf ek awaaz hai.

Rule number do: Agar aapko lage ki koi corridor mein chal raha hai, toh apni flashlight band kar dein. Unhe dikhna nahi chahiye.

Rule number teen: Raat ke 3 baje agar koi aapka naam lekar pukare... bilkul khamosh rahein. Wo sirf check kar raha hai ki aap jaag rahe hain ya nahi. (pause)

Rule number chaar: Agar aapko apne peeche kisi ki saansein mehsoos hon, toh palat kar mat dekhna. Bas apni jagah par ruke rahein.

Aur Rule number paanch... sabse zaroori. Agar koi darwaze ke us paar se kahe 'Kya main andar aa sakta hoon?', toh bas ek baar jawab dein: 'Nahi, abhi niyam ka waqt hai.' (slow)

Ek raat, us guard ne galti se rule number teen ko ignore kar diya. Usne socha shayad koi dost hai jo use ched raha hai. Jaise hi usne apna naam sunne par piche mudna chaha... (beat) Use ehsas hua ki awaaz insaani nahi thi. Wo ek bhayankar, bhari aur ghisiti hui aawaz thi.

Agli subah, guard wahan nahi tha. Sirf uski kursi par wo diary khuli padi thi, jisme naye panno par likha tha: 'Rule number paanch ko todne wale kabhi agle din tak nahi pahunchte.' (slow) Kya aap aaj raat ki shift ke liye taiyaar hain?
