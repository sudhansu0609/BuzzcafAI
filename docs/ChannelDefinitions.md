## 1. Spilled Coffee Studio (Where Stories Are Born)

### Identity & Purpose
*Where stories are born. Not just where they're told.*  
Spilled Coffee Studio establishes the creator's identity as a master writer, storyteller, and story craftsman.

### 5 Content Pillars
1. **Pillar 1 (30%) – Original Work** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐: Premium original short stories, poetry, audiobooks, fantasy, sci-fi, and emotional stories.
2. **Pillar 2 (25%) – Great Literature & Classic Authors**: Analyzing master writers (Edgar Allan Poe, Franz Kafka, O. Henry, Premchand, Rabindranath Tagore, H.P. Lovecraft) using legally appropriate excerpts.
3. **Pillar 3 (20%) – Story Analysis**: Analyzing story craft (e.g. *Why this ending works*, *How Pixar writes emotion*, *How Studio Ghibli creates wonder*, *Five rules of writing dialogue*).
4. **Pillar 4 (15%) – Extraordinary True Stories (Human Narratives)**: Human-centered storytelling (e.g. *The man wrongly imprisoned for 27 years*, *The girl who survived alone in the Amazon*, *A letter that changed history*).
5. **Pillar 5 (10%) – Creative Journey**: Novel writing, worldbuilding, notebook tours, library visits, coffee shop writing sessions.

### Content Release Schedule
- **Monday**: Storytelling Analysis (Pillar 3)
- **Wednesday**: Great Author / Classic Literature (Pillar 2)
- **Friday**: Original Story or Poem (Pillar 1)

### What NOT to do
- ❌ NO simple reading or narrating of modern copyrighted works without permission.
- ❌ NO investigative documentaries (leave for Beyond3Baje).
- ❌ NO pure ghost/horror narrations (leave for After Dark).

## 2. Spilled Coffee After Dark (Horror, Paranormal & Urban Legends)

### Identity & Purpose
Formerly *Raat3Baje*, **Spilled Coffee After Dark** is the primary home for horror narrations, creepy urban legends, paranormal investigations, and real-world mysteries.

### 8 Content Sources
1. **Reddit**: `r/nosleep`, `r/LetsNotMeet`, `r/UnresolvedMysteries`, `r/Paranormal`, `r/HighStrangeness`, `r/Glitch_in_the_Matrix`, `r/UrbanLegends`, `r/Missing411`, `r/Creepy`, `r/Backrooms`, `r/AbandonedPorn`.
2. **Wikipedia Rabbit Holes**: Lists of urban legends, ghost towns, mysterious disappearances, cryptids, unsolved murders, abandoned places.
3. **YouTube Trends**: Evergreen searches (`unexplained`, `creepy`, `mystery`, `disturbing`, `abandoned`, `strange footage`, `paranormal`).
4. **Google News**: Timely mystery searches (`mysterious`, `haunted`, `folklore`, `legend`).
5. **Local Indian Folklore**: Village ghost stories, temple legends, forest myths, river spirits, haunted forts, regional state folklore.
6. **Books**: Paranormal case collections, folklore anthologies.
7. **Podcasts**: Obscure case discussions from mystery podcasts.
8. **Government Archives**: Aviation accident reports, missing persons statistics, declassified documents.

### 4 Content Pillars
- **Pillar 1: True Mysteries** (missing people, strange incidents).
- **Pillar 2: Folklore & Urban Legends** (India and international).
- **Pillar 3: Paranormal & Haunted Locations** (clearly distinguishing claims from verified facts).
- **Pillar 4: Internet Horror & Modern Myths** (creepypastas, analog horror, ARGs).
