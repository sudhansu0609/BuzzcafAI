# Wakmangganchi: Garo Tribe ka Raaz 💀

Kehte hain... ye ek primordial demon tha, ek aisa rakshas jo pahadiyon ko barbad kar deta tha. (pause) Wo badhta gaya aur itna vishaal ho gaya ki uske sharir ka hissa zameen tak phaila hua tha. (normal)
