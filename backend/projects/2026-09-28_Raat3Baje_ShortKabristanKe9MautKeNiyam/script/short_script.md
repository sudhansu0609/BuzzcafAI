# Kabristan ke 9 Maut ke Niyam ⚰️

Ye kahani ek aise security guard ki hai, jise ek sunsan kabristan mein naukri mili. (pause) Use laga ye sirf ek high-paying job hai, lekin uske boss ne use 9 ajeeb rules diye... jo sirf Halloween ki raat ko follow karne the. (normal)

Boss ne kaha: 'In rules ko word-for-word follow karna, warna tumhara agla din nahi aayega.'

Rule number ek: Raat 8:30 baje pahunchna. Ek minute pehle ya baad ka koi option nahi hai.
Rule number do: 9:15 tak office ke andar hi rehna aur security cameras ko bilkul mat dekhna.
Rule number teen: Agar koi rone ki awaaz aaye, toh uska jawab mat dena.
Rule number char: Agar koi darwaze par dastak de, toh bahar mat nikalna.
Rule number paanch: Agar kisi ka naam liya jaye, toh palat kar mat dekhna. (pause)

Ek raat, guard ne ek galti kar di. Use laga ki koi peeche se uska naam pukara hai... aur usne rule number paanch tod diya. (beat) Jaise hi wo muda, use dikha ki koi insaan nahi, balki sirf ek khali qabar thi jahan se hawa ka ek thanda jhonka aaya tha... lekin uski awaaz ab bhi wahan goonj rahi thi.

Agli subah, office khali tha. Guard kahin nahi mila, bas uske cabin mein ek purani diary mili jispe likha tha: 'Rule number nau: Kabhi piche mat mudna.' (slow) Aaj bhi log kehte hain ki us kabristan mein ab do guard hote hain... ek jo zinda hai, aur ek jo hamesha peeche dekhta rehta hai.
