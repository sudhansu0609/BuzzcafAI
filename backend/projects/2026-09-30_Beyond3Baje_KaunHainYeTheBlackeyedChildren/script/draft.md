**V.O. (Narrator):** (slow) Lekin, in reports mein koi "supernatural powers" nahi thi. Koi teleportation nahi thi, koi "glitching" reality nahi thi. Wo bas do bacche the jinhe dekh kar log darr gaye kyunki wo 'unnatural' lag rahe the.

## [SCENE 07: POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS]

**VISUAL:** [Abstract visuals of waves or distorted faces.]

**V.O. (Narrator):** (normal) Agar hum science aur psychology ki nazar se dekhein, toh iske kuch logical explanations ho sakte hain. Sabse pehla hai **Pareidolia**. Hamara dimaag patterns dhundne ke liye bana hai. Jab koi cheez thodi si 'off' hoti hai—jaise ek bacche ki aankhon mein light ka sahi reflection na hona—toh hamara brain use "unnatural" mark kar deta hai.

**V.O. (Narrar):** (slow) Dusra factor hai **Mass Hysteria**. Jab ek area mein kuch log ajeeb cheezon ki baat karte hain, toh wo khabar failti hai aur log darr ke maare unhe "supernatural" maan lete hain.

**V.O. (Narrator):** (normal) Aur teesra... **Evolution of Fear**. Log aksar purani kahaniyon mein naye elements jodte jaate hain taaki wo zyada 'scary' lagein. Ek 1980s ki "strange child" ki report ko 2024 tak aate-aate ek "interdimensional entity" ban gaya.
