# Timeline

- Pre-Modern Era: Oral traditions in Indian rural belts (Himalayas, Western Ghats) regarding 'Echoes of the Past' where travelers heard voices repeating words from days prior.
- 19th Century: Early colonial-era records of forest guards reporting 'phantom sounds' or repetitive calls in dense teak and pine forests.
- Late 20th Century: Reports of radio interference and 'ghost signals' occurring in remote forest outposts during specific atmospheric conditions.
- 2010s - Present: The rise of digital urban legends involving mobile devices, GPS malfunctions, and audio recordings (voice notes) being 'looped' or 'echoed' in isolated locations.
