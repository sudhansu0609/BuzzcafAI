# Shraap Wala Gaon: Jahan Waqt Ruk Gaya ⏳

Ek aisi jagah ki kahani... jahan suraj dhalne ke baad maut ka sannata chha jata hai. (pause) Hum baat kar rahe hain ek aise gaon ki, jo map par toh dikhta hai, par jiske raaste se guzarna aaj bhi log ghabrahat mein darrte hain. (normal)

Ye koi kahani nahi, balki ek 'shrap' ka nateeja hai. Kehte hain saalon pehle, is gaon ke logon ne ek bade anuchit shakti ya kisi rakshak ko naraaz kar diya tha. (pause) Us din se lekar aaj tak, us gaon mein waqt jaise tham gaya hai. (normal)

Imagine kijiye... aap ek sunsan sadak par chal rahe hain aur achanak aapko dikhta hai ek purana gate. Gaon ke ghar wahi hain, lekin unmein koi jaan nahi hai. Log bolte hain ki yahan jo bhi raat ko rukta hai, wo agli subah kabhi nazar nahi aata. (pause) Kuch logon ne dawa kiya hai ki unhe sham ke waqt kisi ke rone ki awaaz aati hai, ya phir aise log dikhte hain jo sadiyon pehle mar chuke hain. (normal)

Logon ka maanna hai ki ye shraap itna gehra hai ki gaon ki mitti ne purani roohon ko apne andar qaid kar liya hai. (pause) Kuch kehte hain ki waqt wahan ruk gaya hai, aur jo log wahan jaate hain, wo us ruke hue waqt ka hissa ban jaate hain. (normal)

Kya aap himmat karenge aise gaon mein kadam rakhne ki? (beat) Jahan har mod par ek purana raaz aapka intezaar kar raha hai. (pause) Kuch jagah sirf zameen ka tukda nahi hoti... kabhi-kabhi wo ek sadiyon purane shrap ka qila hoti hain. (slow) Kya aapko lagta hai ki kuch cheezein behtar hai ki unhe khamosh hi chhoda jaye?
