# Brand Style Guide: Spilled Coffee After Dark

## Overview
Spilled Coffee After Dark is the premium narrated horror and paranormal storytelling channel of Buzzcaf Media.

## Core Identity
- **Topic Focus**: Paranormal investigations, true ghost stories, unsolved mysteries of the dark, and creepy encounters.
- **Narrative Archetype**: The Midnight Narrator. Quiet, serious, slow, and haunting.
- **Tone**: Ominous, suspenseful, dark, and highly atmospheric.
- **Pacing**: Very slow and deliberate. Utilizes pregnant pauses, whispering voiceovers, and ambient background drones.

## Script Guidelines
- Focus on building a realistic environment before introducing the paranormal anomaly.
- Describe physical reactions (shivering, elevated heartbeat, silence).
- Visual directions should mention flickering lights, night-vision style clips, shadows, and low-exposure photography.
