# H.H. Holmes ka Murder Castle 🏰

Chicago, 1890s. Ek aisi building jise 'Murder Castle' kaha jata tha. (pause) Ye koi purani horror movie ki script nahi thi, balki ek real-life nightmare tha jo Chicago ke W. 63rd Street par bana tha. (normal)

Is building ko design kiya tha H.H. Holmes ne—jise 'The Beast of Chicago' bhi kaha jata hai. Holmes koi aam insaan nahi, balki ek doctor aur con artist tha jisne is teen-manzil ki building ko ek maut ke jaal yaani 'death trap' mein badal diya tha. (pause) Usne ise kaise banaya? (normal)

Records ke mutabik, Holmes ne is castle mein secret passages, trapdoors, aur gas vents ka istemaal kiya tha.
