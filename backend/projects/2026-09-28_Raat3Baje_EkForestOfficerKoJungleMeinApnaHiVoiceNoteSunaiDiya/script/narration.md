Jungle Ki Khamosh

Jaise-jaise hamari gaadi Zone 4 ki taraf badhi, jungle ka mahaul puri tarah badal gaya. Charon taraf ghane ped ek deewar ki tarah khade the jo bahar ki duniya ko alag karte the. Baarish ke baad mitti ki bhari aur meethi mahek hawa mein zyada shiddat se mehsoos ho rahi thi. Aisa lag raha tha jaise zameen kuch kehna chahti ho. Hamari gaadi ke pahiya jab geeli pattiyon par chalte, toh unse nikalne wali aawaz bhi kisi gehri khamoshi mein dabi rehti thi. Is ilake mein hamari raftar kaafi dheemi thi aur hawa ek jagah ruk kar sirf dhire se hil rahi thi. Vikram ne apna walkie-talkie uthaya aur uske signal ko check karne ki koshish ki, par wahan sirf ek bhari shor yaani static sunai de raha tha. Maine unse pucha ki kya humein base station se koi khabar mil rahi hai? Unhone bas ek gehri saans li aur apna sar hilaya. Vikram ne thodi gambhirta se kaha, "Is zone mein signal aksar mar jaate hain, yahan se aage hum sirf apne upar nirbhar rahenge." Humne apni raftar nahi badhayi balki savdhani se aage badhte rahe.

Humne gaadi ek jagah roki aur kuch der ke liye wahan ruk kar us mausam ko mehsoos kiya jo kaafi bhari tha. Mausam ki nami itni zyada thi ki saans lete waqt bhi gale mein kuch rukavat jaisa lag raha tha. Vikram ne mujhe dekha aur phir unhone ek khaas raste ki taraf ishara kiya jise wo echo zone kehte the. Unhone bataya ki is valley mein kabhi-kabhi purani aawazein ya kisi janwar ki cheekh ek hi jagah par bar-bar goonjti rehti hain. "Yahan ki hawaein kuch cheezon ko pakad leti hain aur unhe baar-bar repeat karti hain," unhone warn kiya jo sunkar mujhe thoda ajeeb laga. Maine tab unki baat ko sirf ek anubhav maana, lekin dil mein ek halki si bechaini ka ehsaas hone laga tha. Humne gaadi phir se chalu ki aur us raste par nikal pade jo lagbhag mitti ke dheron aur purani jhadion se dhaka hua tha. Har mod par mujhe aisa lag raha tha jaise hum kisi anjaan khatre ki taraf badh rahe hain.

Har kadh par lagta tha jaise koi jhaadiyon ke peeche chhupa ho, jo hamari har harkat ko bade dhyan se track kar raha ho. Mere peeche se aane tung hawaon mein ek thandak thi jo haddion tak mehsoos hoti rahi. Vikram bhi thode alert nazar aaye aur unhone apni gun ki grip ko thoda zyada mazboot pakad liya tha. Humne kuch nahi kaha, par hum dono ko pata tha ki hum is bade jungle mein akele nahi hain. Is sannate mein har choti si aawaz, jaise kisi sukhe patte ka girna ya door kahin ek pathar ka takrana, bahut zyada daravani lagti thi. Koi bhi dhwani hata sa lag rahi thi jo mahol ko aur bhari bana rahi thi.

Jaise-jaise shaam hone lagi, jungle ka rang badalne laga aur sab kuch thoda dhundhla dikha. Suraj ki roshni kam hoti ja rahi thi aur pedon ki parchhaiyan lambi hokar zameen par phail rahi thi. Tabhi humne pehli baar us ajeeb shor ko suna, jo ek dum se mere kaan mein goonj gaya. Maine Vikram ki taraf dekha aur unka chehra bhi us aawaz ki taraf mud gaya tha. "Yeh kya hai?" maine dheere se pucha, meri awaaz hawa mein thodi kaanp gayi thi.
