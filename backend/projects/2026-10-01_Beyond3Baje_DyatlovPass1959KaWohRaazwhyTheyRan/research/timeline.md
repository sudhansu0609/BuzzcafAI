# Timeline

- Late 1958: The group of nine hikers, primarily students and graduates from Yekaterinburg, begins preparation and training for their winter trek.
- January 1959: The group travels by train to the Ural Mountains to begin their expedition.
- February 1, 1959: The hikers reach the area near Kholat Syakhl (Mountain of the Dead) and establish a base camp.
- February 2, 1959: The group sets up their tent at the final location; they are documented as having prepared for extreme conditions.
- Late Night February 2 / Early February 3, 1959: The 'incident' occurs where the hikers flee the tent in a state of distress or panic.
- February 23, 1959: A search party discovers the abandoned tent and the first remains of the group.
- March 1959: Soviet authorities conclude their official investigation into the disappearance.
