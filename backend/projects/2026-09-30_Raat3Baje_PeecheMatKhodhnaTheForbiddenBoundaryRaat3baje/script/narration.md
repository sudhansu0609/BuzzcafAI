## [SCENE 12: GRAM DEVTA]

**SOUND:** [Dark ambient, tanpura drone, low sarangi, 60-70 bpm.]

**V.O. (Narrator):** (normal) Bharat ke gaonon mein, har gaon ka apna ek raksha karne wala devta hota hai — jisse kehte hain **Gram Devta**. Ye koi mandir ka bada devta nahi hota. Ye local hota hai — sirf us gaon ka, sirf uss zameen ka.

**V.O. (Narrator):** Gram Devta ka kaam hota hai gaon ki boundary ki raksha karna — na sirf insaano se, balki un cheezon se jo bahar se andar aana chahti hain. Isi wajah se, aksar gaon ke bahar, ek boundary stone ya chhota shrine banaya jaata hai — jaise ek invisible line, jise paar karne se pehle ijaazat leni padti hai.

**V.O. (Narrator):** (slow) Yahi wajah hai ki hamari kahani mein, Peepal ka ped ek boundary marker ki tarah kaam karta hai. Gram Devta se juda hua ek structure — jise disturb karna, us poori raksha ko tod sakta hai.

**VISUAL:** [Reference imagery: Yantra diagrams, generic. Darker lighting.]

**SOUND:** [Music builds slightly — 75-85 bpm.]

**V.O. (Narrator):** (normal) Ab baat karte hain un entities ki jo is kahani ke peeche hain. Hindu mythology aur rural oral tradition mein do figures baar baar aate hain — **Pishach** aur **Pret**.

**V.O. (Narrator):** (slow) Pishach — ek aisi entity jo insaan nahi hai. Kaha jaata hai ki ye maans-bhakshi hota hai, aur khaas taur par unclean ya unconsecrated zameen se juda hota hai — jaise Masaan, yaani shamshaan ghat.

**V.O. (Narrator):** (normal) Pret, dusri taraf, un aatmaon ko kaha jaata hai jinke antim sanskar poore nahi hue, ya jo violent ya untimely maut mare. Wo bhatakte rehte hain — na is duniya mein, na us duniya mein.

**V.O. (Narrator):** (beat) Masaan ko hamesha ek "forbidden ground" maana gaya hai — ek aisi jagah jo spiritually dangerous hai, jahan ritual caution zaroori hai. Isi wajah se hamari kahani mein, khudai karte waqt "lakdi jaisi cheez" milna — ek sanket hai ki zameen ke neeche kuch aisa hai jo disturb nahi hona chahiye.

## [SCENE 15: SACRED GROVES]

**VISUAL:** [B-roll: dense forest patch, Kerala-style sacred grove imagery, dappled light through trees.]

**SOUND:** [Music softens — dark ambient continues.]

**V.O. (Narrator):** (normal) Ek aur fascinating tradition hai jo isi soch se judi hai — **sacred groves**. Kerala mein inhe kehte hain **Kavu**. Ye jungle ke chhote patch hote hain jo generations se chhue tak nahi jaate, kyunki maana jaata hai ki wahan koi devta ya protector spirit rehta hai.

**V.O. (Narrator):** Ye sirf religious belief nahi hai — ye ek ecological practice bhi hai. Sadiyon se, in groves ne biodiversity ko protect kiya hai, sirf is wajah se ki logon ne unhe "forbidden" maan liya. Darr ne, ek tarah se, nature conservation ka kaam kiya.

**V.O. (Narrator):** (slow) Interesting hai na? Ki jo cheez humein darr lagti hai, wahi cheez kabhi kabhi humein bachati hai — khud se, apni greed se.

**V.O. (Narrator):** (slow) Lekin yahi toh horror ki taakat hai, na? Ki fiction, jab asli darr se judi hoti hai, tab woh sabse zyada chubhti hai.
