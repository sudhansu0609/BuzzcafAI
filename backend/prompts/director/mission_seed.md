## Goals
- Run six channels steadily -- Raat3Baje, Beyond3Baje, Life3Baje, Khayal3Baje, The Tall Storyteller, Originals -- with long videos plus Shorts/reels.
- Content that sounds like me: Hinglish, my own writing style, my fixed greeting said once.

- Stories that are researched (web first, never invented) and layered: specific details, plants, an earned twist.

## Intentions (how he wants it done)
- Every long video opens with a few minutes I record fresh on camera; the rest is my cloned voice. Reels are fully cloned.
- A Raat3Baje long video is a pure story (no analysis), and the five formats take turns: rules, real encounter, fiction, black magic, tantra.
- The Tall Storyteller: mostly detective and comedy, sometimes crime, never horror.
- Life3Baje: vlog + motivational. Vibe reels are hyper-real AI clips with 2-4 ghazal lines, no voice.
- Story channels: pictures and clips only, the same characters, no camera in any shot, no stock pictures on horror.
- Dense visuals; my voice crisp and bassy.
